01 — About Us

A Firm Built on Purpose

White Spark Consulting is a premier organisational transformation advisory firm rooted in East Africa and serving ambitious institutions across the continent and beyond. We combine the rigour of global consulting methodology with an intimate understanding of African institutional dynamics.

03 — SME Focus

East Africa's Backbone:
Small & Medium Enterprises

Dedicated to the Builders
of Tomorrow's Economy

SMEs account for over 90% of businesses in East Africa and contribute the majority of private sector employment. Yet most go underserved by traditional consulting firms priced and structured for large corporations. White Spark Consulting has built a dedicated SME practice — bringing enterprise-grade thinking at a scale and price point that works for growing businesses.
Right-Sized Advisory
Packages and engagement models designed for SME budgets and decision-making cycles — not scaled-down corporate proposals.
Founder-Centred Approach
We work directly with founders and owner-managers, respecting the entrepreneurial DNA while building the systems for scale.
Growth-Stage Expertise
We specialise in the critical 10–500 employee growth stage where structure, culture, and systems determine whether a business survives or scales.
Sector-Specific Tools
From agribusiness to retail tech, fintech to manufacturing — we bring deep sector knowledge and tools calibrated for East African market realities.

07 — East Africa Focus

Rooted in Africa.
Reaching the World.

White Spark Consulting is proudly headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya — the innovation, finance, and business hub of East Africa. Our consultants bring lived experience of East African institutional dynamics, regulatory environments, cultural nuances, and market realities that global firms simply cannot replicate from abroad.

From government reform programs in Rwanda to financial sector transformation in Tanzania, digital infrastructure projects in Uganda, and SME scale-ups in Kenya — we are where the transformation is happening.
