Privacy Notice

Version 2026-09 · Last updated 26 September 2026

White Spark Consulting ("we") is based in Nairobi, Kenya. This notice explains what we collect through this website, why, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

Enquiries you send us

When you use the consultation form we store your name, email, and any optional details you add (phone, organisation, country, industry, timeline, budget, preferred reply channel, your message and any file you attach). We use this only to respond to your enquiry and to manage our relationship with you. Attachments are stored privately and are visible only to authorised staff.

Website analytics

By default we record anonymous page visits: the page viewed, time, referring site, campaign tags, device type, browser, screen size, language and time zone. We link these to a random session identifier that ends when you close the tab. This keeps the site working and helps us spot errors.

If you choose Allow analytics, we also store a random visitor identifier on your device so we can understand return visits and which sections are useful. You can change this at any time using Cookie settings in the footer.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't use advertising trackers or third-party ad cookies.
  • We don't record keystrokes or form contents until you press Send.

Where data is stored

Data is held with our database provider, Supabase, in the European Union (Ireland), protected by access controls so that only authorised White Spark staff can read it. Analytics records are deleted after about 13 months; consent records after two years.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Email info@whitesparkconsulting.com and we will respond within the period required by law. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, Kenya.
