Lead Consultant & Founding Partner

William Ndumia

MSc. Organisational Leadership & Strategy  |  CHRP
LinkedIn ↗

William is the founding force behind White Spark Consulting, bringing over 15 years of deep expertise in organisational transformation, human capital strategy, and institutional reform across East Africa. He has led high-stakes mandates for public sector institutions, multinational corporations, and fast-growing SMEs — building a reputation for both intellectual rigour and practical, results-driven delivery.

His experience spans organisational redesign, change management, leadership development, and AI-enabled HR transformation, with a particular focus on East African institutional contexts. William combines global frameworks with ground-level pragmatism, making complex transformation accessible and sustainable for organisations at every stage.
